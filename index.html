Flashlight Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Flashlight, developed by Appify Innovations, handles information when you use the Flashlight mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Flashlight does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

The application may require access to device features that are necessary for the flashlight functionality.

2. Device Permissions

Flashlight may request permission to access the device camera/flash hardware when necessary to provide the flashlight functionality.

The application does not use the camera to take or store photographs unless explicitly required by a feature of the application.

3. Advertising

Flashlight may use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect and use certain information, such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, and information about interactions with advertisements, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

For more information about how Google uses information from apps that use its services, please visit Google's privacy policy.

4. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services, including Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), to provide advertising functionality.

These third-party services may collect information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the application. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Flashlight is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the Flashlight application, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.
